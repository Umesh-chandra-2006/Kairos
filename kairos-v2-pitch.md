# Kairos — Investor Narrative (One Page)

**Kairos: the daily 90-second spoken interview rep, paid for by the institution that owns the outcome.**

---

## The problem

15 lakh engineering graduates enter India's placement market every year. ~40% get placed. The interview is where it breaks — not on typing answers, but on **speaking under pressure**. Students grind LeetCode and aptitude PDFs, then freeze in a live 20-minute technical round.

Meanwhile, every free tool grades *text*: ChatGPT evaluates a written answer (and flatters it). Google built Interview Warmup to score spoken answers and **retired it in April 2026, pointing users to a generic free chatbot**. Nothing makes a final-year student *speak a timed answer every single day*.

## The wedge

**One company-tagged question a day. A 90-second voice-note answer. An honest grade across content, structure, and delivery. A streak. A countdown to placement season.**

- Duolingo proved the daily rep lifts retention (D7 +14% from streaks alone). Wordle proved the daily ritual scales to millions. No one owns the "one question per day" format in prep — the category is hobbyist-scale.
- The behavior is unowned because it's the highest-friction one in edtech — and that friction is the moat. NxtMock (NxtWave) validates the demand (58k+ AI simulations, 60% Tier-2/3 adoption) but is a practice *library*, not a daily habit.

## The buyer is the college

Students are price-hostile; **placement cells are funded buyers**. They already spend ₹5–15L/yr (Eklavvya), ₹599–1,298/student (AMCAT), and issue live tenders. We sell the daily-practice layer at **₹500–1,500/student/season — under the AMCAT price line**, as an expense a Training & Placement Officer signs without a procurement committee.

- **Contract = retention.** Per-batch, per-season, invoiced up-front. Students churn at placement — the *institution renews every year* with a new final-year cohort.
- **TPO product:** per-student practice completion, readiness heatmaps, placement-outcome reports.
- **Consumer layer for growth:** free (1 voice take/day) + ₹199–399/season paid — virality through the same WhatsApp/Telegram groups where the students already live.

## The moat (a combination, not a feature)

1. **Content:** the only current-season, company×role×round×topic-tagged India campus bank. Freshness is proven decisive (pre-2023 TCS papers actively mislead; Wipro's 2021 format is dead). LLMs cannot fabricate "what TCS asked this season." Sourced by the same community that consumes it — give-to-get gating (~24% contribute at the wall, NBER-validated), ₹50–150 verified-question bounties, campus ambassadors, Telegram mining.
2. **Honesty:** three-band grades from multi-judge sampling + a human-graded calibration set + blind re-scoring + deterministic delivery metrics (the LLM can never hallucinate "you paused 12 times"). The anti-sycophancy brand — the opposite of ChatGPT praising a weak answer.
3. **Data:** an Indian-accent practice corpus (DPDP-safe: raw audio auto-deleted, derived features retained).
4. **Distribution:** institutional contracts + a WhatsApp-native habit loop (CAC ≈ ₹0).

## Traction plan

90-day kill test before scaling: WhatsApp bot + web recorder + 3-band grader + 300 seeded questions + 2 pilot colleges at ₹500/student. Pre-committed gates: D30 voice retention ≥30%, ≥3 takes/week median, ≤15% grade band-flips, ≥90% calibration pass. Both structural risks (will students speak daily? does Indian-accent ASR work on budget Androids?) retired with data for under $5k. On pass: 20-college funnel → ₹1Cr ARR; content flywheel to 15k–20k tagged items; seed raise on proven behavior.

## The ask

Seed (~$1–2M India pre-money, AI+distribution positioning) to run the kill test, sign the first 20 college contracts, and stand up the seasonal content loop.

**Kairos = the coach who makes you show up daily. Paid for by the institution that needs you placed.**
